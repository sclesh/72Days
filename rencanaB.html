HAPPE BIRTHDAE

My friend who quietly slipped into my life

I don't even know what I'm making. I've been working on this since the 29th just for this message. I'm really bad at writing and making things up, and I'm scared my words won't fully express how I feel. That's why I kept delaying it.

So here I am, writing whatever I can. Maybe it's plain and boring, but at least I'm making something in the end, even if it doesn't feel perfect enough.

You said, “I'm sorry I couldn't prepare a well-deserved present in time.”

Nah, you're wrong. This is already more than enough. I never even expected someone would send me a letter like this for my birthday. You're the only one.

I'm not really someone who celebrates birthdays. Honestly, I'm already grateful if someone even remembers it.

But sometimes I'm impressed by people who give small gifts to others on their birthdays. I never really thought about doing things like that before I received small gifts myself, even from people who weren't that close to me.

And said, “and then becoming someone who's important to me.”

I never expected you to think of me that way. I don't really see myself as someone truly important in your life.

We both have our own real lives, especially now that we're getting busier with college and everything.

Maybe our relationship is just sharing stories and experiences online, but honestly, I think that makes sense.

It feels strange lately because I'm too stupid at managing my time, and we don't talk as much anymore. Dunno.

Also said, “I'm surprised at how you found them cool and not weird, unlike others.”

After entering college, I realized that I'm actually someone who likes listening to people talk about their hobbies and interests. It sounds fun and cool to me.

I often bring up hobbies just so conversations can keep going, and I'm happy that this small habit of mine could make you happy too.

Sometimes I also wish I could meet someone like myself.
And maybe I already did.
It's you.

Sometimes I get bored, and then you suddenly appear through chat. Talking about random little things from the videos we send each other is actually really fun.

I also never expected we could get this close(?) even though we only met online.

Just so you know, I'm not this fun in real life unless I'm already close to someone. I'm not an extrovert, just a shy person pretending to be cool.

I hope we can keep being friends even without truly knowing each other's real selves.

Oh yeah, I also like it when you ask me to choose things for you, even though sometimes I don't even know what those things are.

My wishes for you

I hope whatever problems you're dealing with right now will find their way out soon.

I hope you keep meeting good people who support everything you do — your hobbies, your studies, everything.

I've had some struggles this year too, but there was a light that kept my mind sane.

And maybe one part of that light came from your wishes for me in the birthday letter you wrote.

Big thanks.

May I ask something?

Why does the song “Mary on a Cross” remind you of me? Is it because of the vibes? Or something else?

Or maybe you just happened to listen to it while drawing Lucien?

I don't know what song carries your vibes, but the one song that always reminds me of you is “Pretty Girl.”

I honestly don't know what I wrote here, but this is my birthday present for you.
